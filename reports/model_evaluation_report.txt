6. BUSINESS INTERPRETATION
- Recall measures the proportion of actual churners identified.
- Precision measures the proportion of flagged customers who churned.
- False negatives are churned customers the model did not flag.
- ROC-AUC measures ranking quality.
- Average precision summarizes precision-recall performance.
- Lower Brier scores indicate better probability accuracy.

7. LIMITATIONS
- Test metrics are snapshot-based estimates.
- No threshold tuning was performed on test data.
- Feature importance is not causal evidence.
- Correlated features can distort importance.
- Test results must not drive model selection.
- Business deployment needs external validation.
